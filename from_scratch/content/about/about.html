About the StrucEng Library

About the Project

The StrucEngLib is a open source framework for the numerical analysis of engineering structures, being developed at the Chair of Concrete Structures and Bridge Design at ETH Zürich. It offers a variety of material models (linear elastic, ideal plastic, nonlinear), load generators, geometry adapters, verification and plotting tools.

Currently, the library supports the ANSYS Workbench platform with Mechanical as the solver backend, where StrucEngLib features are accessible as an extension. The package and models are packaged as a fully open source Python-based framework aimed at facilitating collaboration between engineering research and industry.
