    The StrucEng Library Project

    The central question is whether visibility-aware geometric supervision can help learned representations capture global spatial structure. The repository currently contains a two-stage pipeline: first, cross-modal latent alignment between images and visible point-cloud graphs; second, localization of latent observations against a larger scene graph.

    Visibility metrics are useful because they depend on long-range spatial relationships. This makes them a natural way to evaluate locality bias and global reasoning in graph neural networks.
